# Escape the Sun: Dubai — Google Play draft listing

**App name:** Escape the Sun: Dubai

**Short description (under 80 characters):** Outsmart Dubai's sun. Race from shade to shade in 20 puzzle levels!

**Full description:**

Dubai is beautiful. Dubai is HOT.

In Escape the Sun: Dubai, every step counts. Navigate sunny streets, time the moving shadows, find a cool metro stop, and reach the next air-conditioned refuge before you overheat.

- 20 handcrafted puzzle challenges set in Old Dubai.
- Learn how to use buildings, palm trees, and safe stations to survive the sun.
- Earn Sun Coins by completing levels, including smaller replay rewards.
- Unlock silly Dubai-inspired characters and equip your favorite.
- Use optional in-game power-ups, or go for the perfect run without them.
- Discover Dubai landmarks and fun facts.
- Play the core puzzle game offline; some landmark photographs and external articles require a connection.

No real-money purchases or advertising are currently included.
