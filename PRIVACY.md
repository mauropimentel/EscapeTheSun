# Escape the Sun: Dubai — Privacy information (draft)

This is a preliminary disclosure for the current test build, **not a final legal privacy policy**.

The current game saves progress, scores, coins, inventory, outfit choices and sound preferences locally on the device using browser storage inside the app. It does not require an account. No analytics or advertising SDK is currently integrated.

Landmark information may load photos and pages from Wikipedia/Wikimedia over the internet. Those requests can disclose standard network information, such as IP address, to the external provider. Cached photos may remain on the device. The game is otherwise intended to work without a connection.

Before public release, confirm all third-party requests, image licenses, Play Console Data safety declarations, contact email and the final published privacy-policy URL.
